gitpulse

a tiny cli that tells you what you actually worked on this week, without git log archaeology. outputs a plain text summary of your commits, grouped by repo.

noisewall

ambient sound mixer for people who can't work in silence or in noise. runs in the browser, no backend, no account. mix rain, white noise, cafe sounds, and lo-fi with independent volume sliders.

sitewatch

monitors websites and sends a discord ping when something changes. checks a list of urls on a cron schedule and diffs the content. dead simple, runs on a $5 vps without breaking a sweat.

tty-music

terminal music player built with rodio and ratatui. plays local files, remembers your queue, shows a tiny waveform visualizer. built it because spotify was too much and mpd was too complicated.

readtime

estimates reading time for markdown and plain text files. pipe into it or give it a path. outputs something like "~4 min read (892 words)". i use it before publishing things here.

about

hey, i'm utkarsh. online i go by vaproh. i'm eighteen and figuring things out.

i study computer science, which mostly means i spend a lot of time on things adjacent to the curriculum — building small tools, reading about operating systems, and occasionally breaking my linux install in new and interesting ways.

this site is my personal corner of the internet. i write about whatever's on my mind: life updates, things i've built, books or games that stuck with me, technical notes, and short thoughts that didn't fit anywhere else.

i don't write to build an audience. i write because it helps me think, and because i like the idea of having a small piece of the internet that's genuinely mine.

what i'm into

software: systems programming, linux internals, cli tools, and occasionally game development. i mostly write rust and go these days, with python for when i'm being lazy.

music: deftones, radiohead, portishead, and a long tail of ambient and electronic stuff. sometimes jazz when i'm working.

games: dark souls series, the last of us, outer wilds (which ruins you for other games). strategy games when i have the patience.

other: photography mostly on my phone, reading (mostly nonfiction, slowly getting back into fiction), coffee.

this site

built with plain html, css, and a handful of lines of javascript. no cms, no framework, no build step. source on github.
